## Caveats and misses

- **#1 is not zero.** 1905 minutes over the limit remain. Hero feeder F7 on Wed 4 Jun goes 90.3 kW / 270 min → 61.7 kW / 0 min (F10 the same day 270 → 0), but F7 is still over on Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri (150–375 min, 2.8–27.3 kWh). Minutes can rise while the overload falls (F7 Mon 285 → 375 min but 79.1 → 27.3 kWh): the LP penalises energy above the limit, so quote kWh over the limit next to minutes.
- **#1 has one baseline, not two.** Only the self-consumption rule ran; the tuned per-home threshold rule was never run.
- **#3** was 82.2 % on the interim weights (replay#380); the fleet-trained LoadNet (coverage 0.81) brings it to 92.0 %, with or without online conformal. Mock-world drill numbers (0.96–0.98) are local diagnostics, not results.
- **#4 on real PV is weak.** On PVDAQ (non-RO) PVNet beats pvlib by only 14 % and its 10–90 band covers 10 % (bands don't transfer; eval#521). Synthetic gains are partly circular: our simulator uses pvlib physics and the same weather. Unseen-site coverage is 0.61. eval#521 and train.py use different test samples of the same 2025 fleet, so their nMAEs differ (0.015/0.036 vs 0.022–0.024); quote eval#521.
- **#6**: eval#521 (fleet-trained M4 on the committed models/) is the comparison to quote; it replaces train#297 and eval#377 (interim weights). Unseen-site PR-AUC drops to 0.56 (train-time 0.67). Soiling AP is 0.29. With the fleet-trained M4 (replay#506) story site 1042's string outage stays `derated` all week (the interim M4 flipped it to sensor_fault).
- **#8**: the alert reduction vs de-duplicated WindMon is small (0.070 vs 0.077). #70's train#300–303 no longer regenerate on current main (drift from #74/#95/#103); #99's train#591–594 (same command, current main) are the numbers to quote; the rule changes only curtailment-vs-fault F1 (0.62 → 0.93), P/R/alerts are identical to the control train#576–579. The large gap on the older stand-in data (run#135: 0.101 vs 0.114, precision 0.85 vs 0.53) is not the comparison to quote: WindMon is much stronger on #22 data. The LightGBM classifier and thresholds still use labels. In the demo replay (replay#506, one week, WP01) M6 gives 0.114 alerts/turbine-day vs 0.698 for #24's WindMon rules (not de-duplicated); quote it as the demo-week number, and train#593/594 as the held-out evaluation. No full confusion matrix extracted, only curtail-vs-fault F1.
- **#9** comes from a local CPU run (no lab run ID). Peak-hour error 0.30 h is no better than persistence (0.28–0.29 h). The county split uses synthetic shares (nMAE 0.061 vs 0.077).
- **#10** ran on our synthetic fleet's voltages, not on the IEEE European LV feeder named in the plan. With tau 0.68–0.90 on the demo feeders, the twin tightens G by up to 6 % (F7 65.5 → 61.8 kW).
- **#11** is an experiment card (merge=never). It uses ERA5 as a perfect NWP (no forecast error), and the simulator is clean, so gains are optimistic.
- **#12 does not transfer to real SCADA.** On CARE Farm A (non-RO) M7 scores 0.40, the same as the baseline (0.39–0.41), with event recall 0. About 88 % of synthetic false alarms are drifts the simulator never ended in failure. Scoring rules were changed after the first results, but the same rules apply to both methods.
