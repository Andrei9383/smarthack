SMARTHACK XI · PPC "24H TO POWER PPC"

Prosumer Sentinel

One energy agent for Romania. It coordinates home batteries on each low-voltage feeder, watches wind turbines, and commits only what healthy, predictable assets can deliver.

Team: Andrei · Stefan · yolooo332 · Tudor · Alexandra

Midday solar is hitting feeder limits, and nobody can say what each asset should be doing

Network operations

  • Rooftop export crosses the limit of an LV feeder at noon.
  • The evening peak is imported hours later.
  • Home batteries optimise alone, so absorption lands where no headroom is needed.

O&M

  • Faults are found after the loss.
  • Rules like "wind > 15 km/h and power < 10 kW" fire on every stop, curtailment and maintenance included.
  • A broken PV string silently shrinks the flexibility a plan relies on.

Shared root cause: no model of what each asset should do given its weather.

Market size and PPC asset figures are unverified; see Assumptions.

The agent runs by itself from data to action, every 15 minutes

PerceiveProsumer + wind SCADA, RO weather, OPCOM prices, GIS. Validated on the way in.
EstimateOur models: PV and load P10/50/90, site health, wind normal behaviour, zone demand.
QualifyEach asset gets one status: eligible, derated, excluded or human review.
DecidePer-feeder LP, P10 commitment, O&M top-K per crew type.
ActBattery schedules, commitments, work orders, alerts, park reports, GIS flags.

Every action is traceable

Audit row: model version, input hash, prediction, threshold, status. Realised vs committed is logged on the next tick.

One qualification contract links every model to every decision

A broken PV string lowers a feeder's committed MW. A repair returns capacity to the plan.

Decisions come from real constraints, and we never sell the nominal number

Per-feeder plan (LP)

Σ (P90 − L10 − charge + discharge) + rest ≤ G + slack
e ≥ customer comfort reserve R
G = τ·Gmax + (1 − τ)·Gconservative

Worst plausible export. Low GIS trust only ever tightens the limit. When surplus exceeds headroom, we show the residual slack.

P10 commitment

committed = (1 − h) · Q10(deliverable)
nominal ≥ available ≥ committed

Conformal-calibrated scenarios replayed through the battery model. h is the telemetry-loss haircut. Realised is measured afterwards, misses published.

O&M triage: capacity is real

score = P(fault) × (lost kWh × price + lost flex MWh × value) × days until next visit

Top-K per day per crew. Sensor faults get a remote check, never a truck. Curtailment gets no work order.

Robust by design: it can say "I'm not sure", and it shows why

Hard situations

  • Stale telemetry, sensor fault, data gap: excluded and removed from the commitment.
  • Wide interval, ensemble disagreement, OOD: human review, no truck.
  • Wrong GIS: low trust tightens the limit.
  • A model slips: its fallback runs, so a panel degrades, never the demo.

Why it decided

  • Expected vs actual band for every site.
  • Status with its reason on every asset.
  • Which sites carried the absorption on a feeder.
  • Audit trail from action back to the prediction.

Robustness drills on the test set: gaps, frozen sensors, unit errors. pending: drill results + run ID

What PPC would run, and what we assumed

Value

  • PPC pays; prosumers opt in free, with a customer-chosen comfort reserve and override at any time.
  • First pilot value: feeder congestion relief.
  • Wind: fewer false alarms; we quote lost MWh and lei per event from simulation, not a fleet-wide saving.
  • Balancing revenue is not assumed.

Assumptions, not verified

  • Battery control is simulated; no hardware was touched.
  • Export remuneration β and compensation rules.
  • Aggregator rules for small assets; distribution/supply data sharing; GDPR consent.
  • Prosumer market figures and PPC wind fleet details.
  • Battery cost and cycle life are inputs, not results.
